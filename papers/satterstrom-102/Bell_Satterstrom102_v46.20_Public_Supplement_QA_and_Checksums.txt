PLAIN-LANGUAGE DEFINITIONS OF INTERNAL AUDIT TERMS

HB-05
A retrospective audit of status and baseline history. Part 1 checked whether each current row followed
the final status rules. Part 2 compared the status a reviewer believed was current with the master
workbook that was actually active at the time.

Seeded positive control
A previously known error deliberately included as a test of whether an audit method can detect the
kind of failure it is supposed to detect.

Structurally protected
The reviewer did not receive a verified baseline and therefore withheld the count decision rather than
guessing. That review was protected from silently changing the numerator.

Coverage-limited or unverifiable
The historical record was insufficient to establish a full comparison. These rows were not counted as
conforming.

Strict numerator
One qualifying primary endpoint may count once per gene. Secondary or creative routes cannot create
duplicate counts.

Freeze
The strict numerator was frozen at 42/102 in v46.20. Any later count change must be recorded as an
explicit post-freeze amendment; silent edits are prohibited.
